✦ Suggested STP Team Rules ✦

1. Be Respectful

Treat all players and team members with respect. No bullying, harassment, or unnecessary arguments.

2. Be Responsible

Complete your assigned tasks and take your role seriously.

3. No Staff Abuse

Do not misuse team permissions, privileges, or authority.

4. Keep STP Information Private

Do not share private team discussions, unreleased content, or confidential information without permission.

5. Report Bugs Honestly

Testers should report bugs clearly and truthfully. Do not intentionally break the game or exploit glitches.

6. No Scamming or Deception

Never scam players or pretend to have powers, items, or authority that you do not actually have.

7. Communicate With the Team

If you have a problem, question, or suggestion, communicate with the team respectfully.

8. Stay Active

Try to remain active according to your role. If you need a break, let the team know.

9. Follow STP Decisions

Respect official decisions made by the STP leadership team.

10. Have Fun! ✦

Help make STP a welcoming, creative, and enjoyable community for everyone.

By applying for the STP Team, you agree to follow these rules.
